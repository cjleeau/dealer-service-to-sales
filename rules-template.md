# Dealer rules

Fill in each line for your dealership, save the file as `rules.md`, and upload it with your bookings each time. Plain English is fine, the AI reads it like a note from you.

Standard new-vehicle warranty by make (years): 
Example: Toyota 5 years, Hyundai 5 years, Kia 7 years.

Major job threshold (scheduled service plus quoted extras, in dollars): 
Example: $800.

Trade-up points (age and kilometres): 
Example: 5 years old or 100,000 km.

Brands and models we sell: 
Example: New Hyundai, plus used vehicles of all makes.

Fuel step ups we are happy to suggest: 
Example: Petrol or diesel to hybrid or electric, hybrid to electric.

Business, fleet and novated lease vehicles (include or exclude): 
Example: Exclude.

Anyone or anything else to always leave out: 
Example: Staff vehicles, vehicles booked in by insurers.
